Welcome

A Quiet Corner of the Hills

Mar Me Yullong Farmstay is a cozy home tucked into the green hills of Bijanbari, in the Darjeeling district. Here the days move slowly: morning mist over the valley, tea on the veranda, and the sound of the river drifting up from below.

We are a working farm in a small village, so what you see is real. Come to walk the hill paths, eat what is grown around you, meet the people who live here, and spend a few days away from the noise.

Seasons

When To Visit

The hills change with every season, so there is no wrong time to come.

Spring

March – May

Mild weather, blooming orchids and wildflowers, and lots of birdsong.

Monsoon

June – September

Everything turns lush green. Misty hills and full rivers. Pack rain gear.

Autumn

October – November

Often the clearest skies and sharpest mountain views of the year.

Winter

December – February

Cold, crisp days, warm blankets and long evenings by the fire.
